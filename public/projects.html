bubbyshosting.uk

Free Minecraft server hosting for my friends, running entirely on hardware at home. People sign up on a dashboard, get credits, and spin up their own server in a couple of clicks.

Behind it: a Pterodactyl panel with two game nodes, CtrlPanel for sign-ups and credits, Cloudflare Tunnel so nothing on my network is exposed, and a small script that keeps the Minecraft address pointing at my home connection.
